The game

Shoot, loot, and survive in an evolving universe.

Eclipse puts the feel of a classic shooter underneath an RPG. Every crate, corpse and locker in the world can be searched, and what you pull out of it is what you carry into the next fight: weapons, armor, ammo and gear that actually change how you play.

The world doesn't reset between matches. Territory shifts, enemies move in, and the places you cleared last time may not be clear now. No two drops into Eclipse play the same way, and the gunplay is tight enough that you'll want to keep going back.

Features

What you're getting into.

Loot everything

Containers, bodies, caches and gear left behind in the field can all be searched. Build your kit from what the world gives you.

Gunplay that feels right

Snappy movement, readable recoil, and weapons with real weight behind them. Every gun handles differently, and every hit lands.

Low-poly on purpose

Flat shading and pixel textures by design, with modern lighting, fog and draw distance so the scale actually lands.

An evolving world

The map changes between matches. Factions take ground, new threats spawn in, and the loot moves with them. Nothing stays cleared for long.

Dynamic matches

Objectives, enemy pressure and the state of the world shift mid-match. Plans break, and the game rewards you for adapting on the fly.

Gear that matters

Your loadout is your build. Armor, weapons and attachments change your stats and your options, so what you carry shapes how you fight.
